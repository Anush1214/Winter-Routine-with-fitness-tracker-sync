## 📱 How to Install on Android

1. Download the [Direct APK Link](https://github.com/Anush1214/Winter-Routine-with-fitness-tracker-sync/releases/latest/download/app-release.apk) on your phone.
2. Open **Downloads** $\rightarrow$ Tap **`app-release.apk`** $\rightarrow$ Tap **Install**.
   - *If prompted with "Install unknown apps", tap **Settings $\rightarrow$ Allow from this source** $\rightarrow$ Tap **Install**.*
   - *If Google Play Protect shows a prompt, tap **Install anyway**.*
3. Open the app $\rightarrow$ Sign in or tap **`CONTINUE AS GUEST SHADOW HUNTER`**!

## 🍎 How to Install on iOS (iPhone)

### ⚡ Option 1: Instant 1-Tap PWA Install (No Mac Needed)
1. Open the hosted web app link in **Safari** on your iPhone.
2. Tap the **Share Button** $\rightarrow$ Tap **"Add to Home Screen"** $\rightarrow$ Tap **Add**.
3. The app will launch in full screen with offline support!

### 💻 Option 2: Build Native iOS App in Xcode
```powershell
cd mobile
flutter pub get
cd ios ; pod install ; cd ..
flutter run -d ios
```

## ⌚ Nothing X (CMF Watch Pro 2) Auto-Sync Guide

1. Open the **Nothing X** app $\rightarrow$ **Settings** $\rightarrow$ Enable **Google Health Connect** (Android) or **Apple Health** (iOS).
2. Inside the Winter Arc app $\rightarrow$ Tap **Watch Sync**.
3. Tap **`FETCH LIVE METRICS FROM NOTHING X`**.
4. Tap **`PUSH TELEMETRY & AUTO-CHECK QUESTS`** to auto-complete your daily steps, sleep, and workout goals!
